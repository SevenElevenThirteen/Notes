## ethics
- the rightness or wrongness of actions
- the goodness or badness of results or states of affairs
- the virtues and vices of agants

Convention vs Ethics: what people value vs what shold value

types of ethics:
- descriptive
    研究人们实际的伦理观是什么
- metaethics
    meta-ethics: above ethics
    analyse the meaning of ethics. e.g. 什么是善恶
- normative ethics
    what to do 

## ethics and science

assessments:
+ upstream prior to research (e.g. should it be funded)
+ midstream during research (e.g. how should it be conducted)
+ downstream after research
